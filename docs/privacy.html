Privacy Policy

For the Ajiomart Android app · Effective October 26, 2026

This policy explains what information the Ajiomart shopping app handles, why it is used, and which service providers receive it. We do not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising.

Information the app handles

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, and authentication details when you create or use an account. Supabase Auth handles passwords; Ajiomart does not store your password in its product database.
  • Shopping and order information: products, selected options, quantities, cart, order total, order status, delivery address, recipient name and phone, courier tracking, and payment status or transaction references.
  • Support information: messages you choose to send to Ajiomart by email or WhatsApp.
  • Postal-code lookup: a postal code you enter when you ask the app to check delivery-area details.
  • Administrator uploads: authorized store administrators may choose product photos from the device and upload them to the product catalog.
  • Local preferences: wishlist selections are saved on your device. They are not uploaded to your account.

The app does not request access to your contacts or precise location. Service providers that operate authentication, payments, delivery, or communications may process connection, device, security, or transaction details under their own privacy notices.

How we use information

We use account information to authenticate you and manage your profile; shopping and order information to save your cart, accept and fulfil orders, arrange delivery, show order history, and provide support; and postal codes to return locality details for a delivery check. We use administrator uploads to display products. We use information only for operating and protecting the store, meeting legal or accounting duties, and responding to you.

Service providers and sharing

We share only the information needed for the service you use. These providers process information to provide their services and may handle it under their own privacy terms.

ProviderInformation and purpose
SupabaseAccount authentication, profile, cart, orders, product images, and backend functions used to operate the store.
Razorpay and your chosen payment providerCustomer name, email or phone where provided, order amount and reference, and checkout details to process payment. Ajiomart receives payment status and transaction references; it does not store card, UPI, or banking credentials.

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and security of customers or Ajiomart.

Storage, security, and retention

Account, cart, and order records are stored in the Ajiomart Supabase project. Wishlist preferences are stored locally on your device. Data is transmitted to our services over encrypted HTTPS connections, and Supabase access controls restrict account records to the account owner and authorized store administrators. We retain order records for as long as needed to complete orders, help customers, and meet legal or accounting requirements.

Account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data from the app's Account → Delete account option, or by emailing care@ajiomart.com from your registered email address. The in-app option opens an email draft; send it to submit your request. Include your account email so our support team can verify it. You can submit a request even if the app is no longer installed. After verification, we delete the account and associated personal data; we may retain order records where required for legal or accounting purposes. Wishlist data stored only on your device can be removed by clearing the app's local data or uninstalling the app.

Children

The app is a general shopping service and is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided information can contact us to request its removal.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact care@ajiomart.com.
